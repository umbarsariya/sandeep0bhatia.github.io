Privacy policy

Story Saga (Kid Stories) · Last updated 17 October 2026

Nothing from viewers

We collect no information from children, parents or anyone who watches the channel.

One account only

The app signs in to a single YouTube channel, owned by its creator, to upload the daily story.

Never sold or shared

Google data is never sold, shared with others or used for advertising.

Story Saga (Kid Stories) is a YouTube channel that publishes a new animated bedtime story for children every day. The stories are produced and uploaded by a small app run by the channel's creator, called the Story Saga publishing app below. This policy explains what Google user data that app uses, and why.

Who signs in

Only the channel's owner signs in, once, to let the app upload to the Story Saga channel. The app has no other users, no accounts and no public sign-in.

Google data the app uses

With the owner's permission, the app uses the YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to:

  • upload each day's story and set its title, description, tags and thumbnail;
  • list the channel's recent uploads, so it never publishes the same day twice or repeats a title;
  • create and update the channel's playlists, such as "Mo & Friends: All Stories";
  • read the channel's own analytics reports, to learn which stories viewers enjoy most.

It does not read the owner's email, contacts, files or any data outside YouTube, and it does not access other people's channels or any information about viewers.

How the data is stored

The sign-in token that grants this access is kept as an encrypted secret in the app's private deployment and on the owner's own computer. Channel information the app reads is used only while the day's story is being published and is not stored in any database.

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. The app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements, and the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's own handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

Every Story Saga video is made for children and is marked "made for kids" on YouTube, which limits data collection and features on YouTube's side. Story Saga itself collects no information from children or any other viewer, and this website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or tracking scripts.

Revoking access

The owner can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The stored token stops working as soon as access is removed.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Questions can be raised by opening an issue.
