Saga Stories

A new animated bedtime story for children every day.

Saga Stories is a small personal project. Each day it writes a short story with a gentle moral, illustrates and animates it with AI tools, narrates it, and publishes the finished video to its own YouTube channel, Story Saga (Kid Stories).

The Saga Stories app uses Google sign-in only so it can upload to that one channel, which belongs to the app's owner. It does not ask anyone else to sign in, and it does not collect information about viewers.
