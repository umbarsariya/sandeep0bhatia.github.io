Meet the friends

Mo & Friends

Six best friends with their own colours, voices and big feelings. Any one of them can be the star of tonight's story.

Mo

Baby red panda · the leader

Endlessly curious and brave. Mo always asks "what if?" and is first to set off on an adventure.

Luna

Lavender bunny · the dreamer

Gentle and imaginative. Luna loves stars, songs and making up stories of her own.

Ziggy

Baby chameleon · the joker

Silly, giggly and full of jokes. His cheeks glow rainbow colours with his feelings.

Ollie

Baby owl · the thinker

A thoughtful little know-it-all who is learning it's okay not to know everything.

Coco

Baby elephant · the big heart

Big-hearted and gentle, the strongest of the friends and always the first to give a hug.

Bolt

Duckling · the speedster

Zippy and impatient, always racing ahead, and learning to slow down and listen.
